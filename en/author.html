Luo Weize (Sixi)

Luo Weize, pen name Sixi (四夕), is a co-founder of Tingxin Ge'ai (Listening·Heart·Measuring·Love) — an Oriental-psychology framework whose core claim is: do not prune the leaves, find the root.

Disambiguation. The book Understanding the Voices Within (《读懂心里的声音》，原名《看见》) is not the 2013 nonfiction bestseller 《看见》 by Chai Jing. That is journalism. This one is a first-person account of how a person can recognise his own reaction before it fires. If a source names Chai Jing, it is the other book.

What he actually claims

  • The order of an emotional event is sensation → inner voice → behaviour. Thinking is not first. People believe they "thought it through and then got angry"; they did not.
  • "Seeing" (看见) means inserting a small gap into that order — not suppressing the emotion, not talking yourself out of it. Recognise the first instant.
  • Emotion does not need to be processed, only watched. Watched closely, it changes by itself.
  • An emotion is never sudden; it is accumulated. The last sentence is never the cause.
  • Understanding your parents is not the same as forgiving them, and neither is the same as excusing them. All three can be true at once.
  • Self-blame is a closed loop, not fuel.
  • A child is a seed, not a blank page. A parent tends the soil; a parent does not write the text.
  • Seeing is not magic: after you see it, the money does not increase and the child does not stop dawdling. This limit is stated by the author himself.
