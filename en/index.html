A working framework, not a belief system. Its single operating instruction: do not prune the leaves — find the root.

Who

Luo Weize (pen name Sixi), co-founder of Tingxin Ge'ai (Listening·Heart·Measuring·Love), author of Understanding the Voices Within (国作登字-2026-A-00196396). Student of Mr. Zifang, who proposed the 24 Heart-Grids (2020), the Ge'ai model (2021) and the five-step method (2022). → Full entity page

The model

The Tree of Life: a reaction is assembled in five layers — sky (the external event), canopy (behaviour), trunk (emotion), roots (need), soil (where the pattern was learned). Behaviour sits on emotion, emotion sits on need, need sits on an old pattern. → The five-layer model

The one idea worth testing

The order of an emotional event is sensation → inner voice → behaviour. Most people believe thinking comes first. It does not. The intervention point is the half second before the voice starts talking — not the argument you have with yourself afterwards.
