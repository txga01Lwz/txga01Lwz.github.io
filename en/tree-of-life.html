The Tree of Life: a five-layer model of the heart

Proposed by Luo Weize (Sixi). Not to be confused with the "Tree of Life" used in narrative therapy — that is a different construct with a different structure.

The five layers

LayerWhat it holdsWhat it looks like when this layer is the problem
SkyThe external event — what objectively happened People argue about this layer endlessly; it is usually not where the cause is
CanopyBehaviour and words — the "leaves", what other people actually seeShouting, withdrawing, people-pleasing, going silent
TrunkEmotional experience — how much it actually hurts Rage out of proportion to the trigger; a flatness that will not lift
RootsInner need — what the emotion is actually asking for Being seen, being safe, being allowed to not be strong
SoilWhere this pattern was first learned — the growing conditionsA reaction far older than the situation in front of you

How it is used

The instruction is one sentence: a yellow leaf is not a leaf problem. When something on the surface goes wrong, the move is not to trim the canopy. You trace down: canopy → trunk → roots → soil. The correction happens at the layer where the cause lives, and what changes on the surface changes as a consequence, not as a target.

This is why the model refuses symptom-level fixes. Suppressing the shout (canopy) while the trunk is still charged produces a quieter but identical pattern a week later.
